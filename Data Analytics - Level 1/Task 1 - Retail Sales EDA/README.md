## Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify sales patterns, customer purchasing behaviour, product category performance, and revenue trends.

The objective is to extract meaningful business insights from transaction data and provide actionable recommendations to improve business performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains 1,000 retail transactions with 9 columns.

Key attributes include:
- Transaction ID
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount
- Date

## Project Workflow

1. Data Loading and Initial Inspection
2. Data Cleaning and Preparation
3. Descriptive Statistical Analysis
4. Monthly and Quarterly Sales Trend Analysis
5. Customer Demographics Analysis
6. Product and Category Performance Analysis
7. Correlation Analysis
8. Business Insights and Recommendations

## Key Findings

- The dataset contains 1,000 transactions with no missing values or duplicate rows.
- Total revenue generated was 456,000.
- Premium-priced products contributed approximately 88% of total revenue.
- May recorded the highest monthly revenue.
- September showed a noticeable decline in sales.
- Customer purchasing preferences varied across age groups and genders.
- Saturday recorded the highest sales among weekdays.

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus on premium products to improve revenue.
2. Introduce promotional campaigns during low-performing months.
3. Target customers based on age group, gender, and product preferences.
4. Introduce midweek offers to improve slower sales days.
5. Collect more detailed customer and product information for future analysis.
