## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Google Play Store applications to understand app market trends, user ratings, downloads, pricing strategies, and customer sentiment.

The objective is to extract meaningful insights from app-related data and understand what makes applications successful in the Google Play Store.

## Objectives
- Analyse app categories and market distribution.
- Explore app ratings and user reviews.
- Study the relationship between app size and installs.
- Analyse free vs paid applications.
- Estimate revenue generated by paid applications.
- Perform sentiment analysis on user reviews.
- Identify important business insights for app developers.

## Key Insights

- FAMILY, GAME, and TOOLS are among the largest app categories.
- The average app rating is approximately 4.17.
- Around 92.2% of applications are free.
- Free applications have significantly higher median installs than paid applications.
- App size has a positive but moderate relationship with installs.
- Paid app revenue is highly concentrated among a small number of successful applications.
- Education, Photography, Shopping, and Game categories show strong median install figures.

## Conclusion

This analysis demonstrates how data analytics can help understand the Google Play Store market, user preferences, app performance, and pricing strategies.

The project provides useful insights for developers who want to make data-driven decisions while launching new applications.
